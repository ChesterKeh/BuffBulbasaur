Questions

Good to know

How is this different from an influencer agency?

Agencies sell reach. We sell fit: two businesses whose customers, values and timing line up, so the collab feels natural and both sides gain.

Do I need a big budget?

No. Many collabs cost the partners very little beyond product and time. Our fee is the main cost, and the Warm Intro is a low-risk place to start.

How far ahead should we plan a festive collab?

Two to four months. For Lunar New Year, start the conversation in October or November.

What happens after you introduce us?

On a Warm Intro, you take it from there (we're a message away). On a Sprint, we stay with you through terms, timeline and launch.
