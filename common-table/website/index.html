      Questions

      Good to know

      How is this different from an influencer agency?+

      Agencies sell reach. We sell fit: two businesses whose customers, values and timing line up, so the collab feels natural and both sides benefit.

      Do I need a big budget?+

      No. Many collabs cost the partners little beyond product and time. The Warm Intro is a low-risk way to start.

      How far ahead should we plan?+

      Two to four months for a festive drop. For Lunar New Year, start the conversation in October or November.

      What stops the other side going around you?+

      Every introduction comes with a simple written agreement, so everyone knows the terms before names are shared.
