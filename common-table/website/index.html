No.03 · 13:00 · How it works

From hello to launch day in four steps.

  1. STEP 01 · 30 MIN · FREE

    Coffee chat

    We learn your story, your customers and what a good outcome looks like for you.

  2. STEP 02 · ~1 WEEK

    The match

    We shortlist partners whose audience, values and timing genuinely fit yours. Fit matters more than follower count.

  3. STEP 03 · 1–2 WEEKS

    Warm intro

    A personal introduction, never a cold email. Then we shape the idea, the split and who does what.

  4. STEP 04 · 2–6 WEEKS

    Launch

    Timeline, content and launch-day coordination. Then a wrap-up on what worked and what's next.

No.07 · 21:00 · Questions

Before you go…

How is this different from an influencer agency?+

Agencies sell reach. We sell fit: two businesses whose customers, values and timing line up, so the collab feels natural and both sides benefit.

Do I need a big budget?+

No. Many collabs cost the partners little beyond product and time. The Warm Intro is a low-risk place to start.

How far ahead should we plan a festive collab?+

Two to four months. For Lunar New Year, start the conversation in October or November.

What if the other side goes around you?+

Every intro comes with a simple written agreement, so everyone knows the terms before names are shared.
